IN THE HIGH COURT OF KENYA AT NAIROBI
CONSTITUTIONAL PETITION NO. E229 OF 2022
VICTOR BUOGA v INDEPENDENT ELECTORAL AND BOUNDARIES COMMISSION & OTHERS [2022] eKLR

HOLDING AND DECLARATION ON EDUCATIONAL QUALIFICATIONS FOR COUNTY ASSEMBLY:

1. The Petitioner challenged the constitutionality of Section 22(1)(b) of the Elections Act, No. 24 of 2011, which required candidates seeking election as Member of County Assembly (MCA) to possess a university degree from a recognized institution.
2. The Court held that Article 38(3)(c) of the Constitution guarantees every adult Kenyan citizen the right to be a candidate for public office without unreasonable restrictions.
3. Requiring an undergraduate degree for County Assembly aspirants in an electoral environment where university education is not universally accessible is discriminatory, disproportionate, and unconstitutional.
4. The requirement for a university degree is valid and reserved strictly for Presidential and Gubernatorial candidates under Article 180 and Article 137.
5. Consequently, Section 22(1)(b) of the Elections Act in so far as it demands a university degree for MCAs was declared unconstitutional, null and void. Any registered voter aged 18 and above who meets the general requirements of Chapter Six and Article 193 is eligible to vie for County Assembly without a degree.
