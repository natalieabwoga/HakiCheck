IN THE COURT OF APPEAL AT NAIROBI
CIVIL APPEAL NO. 105 OF 2017
INDEPENDENT ELECTORAL AND BOUNDARIES COMMISSION v MAINA KIAI & 5 OTHERS [2017] eKLR

RATIO DECIDENDI AND HOLDING ON FINALITY OF POLLING STATION RESULTS:

1. The polling station is the true locus of the free expression of the sovereign will of the people of Kenya under Article 1 and Article 38 of the Constitution.
2. Under Article 86 of the Constitution and the Elections Act regulations, the Presiding Officer counts the physical ballots openly in the presence of political party agents, candidates, and observers immediately upon the close of polls.
3. The results declared by the Presiding Officer at the polling station and recorded on Form 34A are final and conclusive.
4. Neither the Constituency Returning Officer nor the Chairperson of the Commission acting as the National Returning Officer has the legal authority or jurisdiction to alter, amend, vary, correct or adjust the presidential election results declared at the polling station.
5. Any variation of the results recorded at the polling station can only be ordered by the Supreme Court sitting as an election court in a formal presidential election petition under Article 140 of the Constitution.
